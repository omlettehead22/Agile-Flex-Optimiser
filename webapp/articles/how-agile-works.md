Most electricity tariffs charge the same price per unit all day. Octopus Agile doesn't. It splits each day into 48 half-hour slots, and each slot has its own price.

## Where the prices come from

Agile prices follow the wholesale electricity market, the price suppliers pay for power a day ahead. When demand is low or wind and solar output is high, wholesale prices fall, and so do Agile prices. When everyone gets home, switches on the oven and the heating, demand peaks and prices rise.

Octopus publishes the next day's prices every afternoon, usually around 4pm. That gives you time to plan the evening and night ahead.

## A typical day

- **Overnight (roughly midnight to 6am):** usually cheap. Demand is low.
- **Morning:** prices rise as people wake up.
- **Middle of the day:** often cheaper again on sunny or windy days, when solar and wind output is high.
- **4pm to 7pm:** the peak. Agile prices are usually at their highest here, often well above a standard tariff.
- **Late evening:** prices fall back.

Every day is different. A windy weekend can bring very low prices; a cold, still weekday evening can be expensive.

## Who it suits

Agile tends to work well if you can move a good share of your use away from the 4pm to 7pm peak:

- You have an **electric car** you can charge overnight.
- You have a **home battery**, which can fill up when power is cheap and run the house through the peak.
- You run the **washing machine, dishwasher or tumble dryer** on a timer.
- You're often **home in the day** and can use power when it's cheap.

It suits you less if most of your use falls in the early evening and can't move, for example cooking and heating for a family between 5pm and 7pm. In that case a fixed tariff or a simpler time-of-use tariff may cost less.

## Things to know before switching

- You need a **smart meter** that sends half-hourly readings.
- There's still a **daily standing charge**, as with any tariff.
- Prices are **variable**: in a wholesale price spike, your bill can go up. Agile has a price cap per unit, but it's well above typical prices.
